# Teams and settlements

Merchant owners can invite dashboard users by email, limit each invitation to selected countries, assign roles, and optionally set an invitation expiry. Invitations remain pending until accepted or declined. New invitees can create an account as part of accepting.

Roles cover Administrator, Settlements, Technical Administration, Batch Disbursements, Finances, Transactions, User Management, and Top Ups. Country-scoped roles only expose the selected country data. An invitee without an account follows the invitation link, creates an account with the invited email, signs in, then accepts or declines the invitation.

Merchant settlement bank details include account holder, account number, bank, SWIFT/BIC, branch address, branch name, sort code, country, and currency. Submitted accounts remain pending until an administrator reviews them. Account numbers are encrypted at rest and masked in dashboard responses.
